About

I started writing songs as a kid, mostly keeping them to myself. In my teenage years, I played in bands and began making my own music. Back then, I enjoyed the process of putting songs together, figuring out how words, melodies, and rhythms fit. When I moved to the US, I started busking and sharing my songs more openly.

My music draws from different genres, cultures, and experiences, so I've never felt the need to stick to one particular sound. I'm influenced as much by the places I've been and the people I've met as by the music I listen to. Making music for me, is simply a way to make sense of life and share a bit of it with others.
